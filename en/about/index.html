WHERE IT ALL STARTED

First a robot. Then a whole new world.

My interest in programming began with LEGO Mindstorms NXT. Suddenly, technology could respond to my own ideas. At twelve, I started exploring C++; later, I taught myself Batch and Java and experimented with Raspberry Pis.

That curiosity has stayed with me. I still build software in my spare time because technical challenges interest me. Music gives me another way to switch gears.

“
Software should make everyday tasks easier. Its complexity shouldn’t become a burden for the people using it.

Julian Tracht

WHAT DRIVES ME

Digitalisation can be better.

Everyday life is full of digital systems that are unnecessarily complicated, unstable or cumbersome. I want to help turn them into understandable, reliable solutions. That takes clear requirements, suitable architecture, thoughtful usability and meaningful tests.
