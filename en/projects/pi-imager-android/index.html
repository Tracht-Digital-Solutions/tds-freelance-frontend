Pi Imager for Android

My free Android app that writes Raspberry Pi images over USB OTG, with no PC and no root.

Project

The app brings the Raspberry Pi Imager workflow to the phone: choose an operating system from the official catalog or a local file, set up the first boot, and write to an SD card in a USB reader or to a USB stick. It is independent and not an official product of Raspberry Pi Ltd.

What is special

A native interface in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose replaces the Qt interface, and Android's USB host with libaums replaces drive access. The app writes first-boot setup directly into the image file's FAT32 boot partition with no root, for cloud-init, systemd and rpi-preseed. Preparation and writing continue as foreground services. A pipeline picks up new stable upstream releases automatically and builds a signed APK.

Safety and testing

Catalog images are checked by SHA-256, and after writing the app reads every sector back. The app is tested on Android emulators, including with a complete official image. Physical USB card readers and the first boot on a Pi are still outstanding.

Own project

Version 0.6.3, tested on emulators, USB hardware still open
